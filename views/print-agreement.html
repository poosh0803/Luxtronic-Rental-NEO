4. Use of Equipment

The Lessee agrees to use the Equipment only for its intended purpose and in a reasonable way. Specifically, the Lessee must:

  • Use the Equipment solely for lawful purposes.
  • Not modify, tamper with, or alter the Equipment (including overclocking, changing BIOS settings, or making hardware customizations) without the Lessor's prior written consent.
  • Not expose the Equipment to conditions or actions that could reasonably cause damage, such as extreme heat, liquids, physical impacts, or installation of unauthorized or incompatible software.
  • Follow all manufacturer instructions, safety guidelines, and any specific advice provided by the Lessor (e.g., avoiding software that may conflict with the Equipment, like certain game servers or anti-cheat tools that could lead to hardware issues such as motherboard blocking).
